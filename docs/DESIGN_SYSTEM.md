# Ohmlab Design System

**Identity: lab notebook at night.** Graphite paper with a faint graticule and grain, a serif hand for titles, copper for the things you act on, and signals that glow only because they carry meaning (current, a lit LED, a hotspot). Controls never glow; they respond with value and edge changes. Why and how it got here: `docs/DESIGN_DIRECTION.md`.

All values live in `src/styles/tokens.css`. Never write a raw hex value anywhere else. Translucent variants are made with `color-mix(in srgb, var(--token) N%, transparent)`.

## 1. Colour roles

| Token | Role | Allowed | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | Page background, scope screens (formula, bench SVG), meter wells | Under the 40 px graticule (`--bg-grid`, 3.5 %) | As a panel fill |
| `--surface-1/2/3` | One cool graphite family (hue ~214°), opaque, lowest to highest | `1` panels and cards, `2` raised/hover and buttons, `3` selected states | Text; translucency or blur |
| `--border`, `--border-strong` | Panel edges; strong = interactive or selected | Every panel has a 1 px border | Thicker than 1 px, except 2–3 px signal accents |
| `--text` | Body text, headings, values | Everywhere | — |
| `--text-muted` | Secondary copy, labels, captions | Taglines, `dt`, help text | Body paragraphs |
| `--text-faint` | Tertiary: day numbers, footer, "Coming soon" tiles | ≥ 12 px only | Anything the learner must read to continue |
| `--brand` `#c98a5e`, `--brand-strong` `#e0a77f`, `--brand-ink` `#1a0f08` | Copper, the one accent: primary actions, links, current position, progress, focus, selection | Primary buttons (solid), links and eyebrows (`-strong`), `aria-current` edges, rings, progress bars, focus ring | Electrical quantities; glows |
| `--sig-current` (cyan `#5cc6dd`) | **Current only**: flow dots, current readouts, active walkthrough step (it traces current in the lab), "on" switch | Diagrams, meters, walkthrough highlights | Decoration unrelated to current |
| `--sig-voltage` (amber `#e2b552`) | **Voltage only**: sources, voltage readouts, walkthrough maths, objective bullets (bolt icon) | Diagrams, meters, maths | Warnings (use `--warn`) |
| `--sig-power` (coral `#e9746a`) | **Power/heat**: power readouts, streak flame, open-circuit endpoints | Meters, heat states | Errors (use `--danger`) |
| `--sig-resistance` (steel blue `#8ea9cc`, like the kit's metal-film resistor) | Resistor bodies, resistor value pickers | Diagrams | UI chrome |
| `--sig-ground` | Wires and ground | Diagrams, 404 trace | — |
| `--ok`, `--warn`, `--danger`, `--info` | Feedback: done/complete, careful, destructive/burnt, neutral note | Callouts, completion states, Reset button, burnt LED | As brand colour |

All signal and feedback colours are below 75 % saturation and pass AA as text on every surface. No purple anywhere. Rules: one signal colour = one meaning, in diagrams, meters and text alike. Never put two signal colours on the same element. A red LED glows in `--danger` because that *is* its light; that is the only physical-colour exception.

## 2. Type

| Use | Family | Size token | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero line | Instrument Serif (`--font-display`) | `clamp(3rem, 7vw, 5.75rem)` (hero only) | 400, −0.02em, line-height 0.98; the one italic word in copper |
| Wordmark | display | 1.75rem | 400; "*lab*" italic in `--brand-strong` |
| Page `h1` | display | `--fs-3xl` (`clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 3.75rem)`) | 400 |
| Section and step `h2`, phase titles | display | `--fs-2xl` (36 px); panel and dialog `h2` 30 px; topic and achievement titles 22–24 px | 400 |
| Part names (`.part-head h1`) | IBM Plex Sans | `clamp(2rem, 3.6vw, 2.75rem)` | 600, −0.02em (model numbers need an unambiguous 1/l) |
| Card and UI titles `h3`/`h4`, buttons, nav, labels | IBM Plex Sans (`--font-body`) | `--fs-sm` … `--fs-lg` | 500–600 |
| Body | Plex Sans | `--fs-md`; lesson prose 17 px, line-height 1.7, max 65 ch | 400 |
| **Every measured value, unit, code, step-number disc** | IBM Plex Mono (`--font-mono`; Plex Sans backs up Ω) | `--fs-xs` … `--fs-2xl` | 400 / 500, `tabular-nums` |

Instrument Serif has one weight, so `font-synthesis-weight: none` and every display use is 400; it is never set below 22 px. Headings use `text-wrap: balance`, paragraphs `pretty`. Labels are sentence case and in Plex Sans; mono is only for numbers, units and code (`.num` gives Plex Sans tabular figures to labels like "Day 4" or "0 / 6 complete"). No uppercase or letter-spaced labels.

## 3. Space, shape, depth

- Spacing scale `--sp-1…7` (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 px). Panels pad `--sp-5` (24 px), `--sp-4` on phones. Page sections are separated by `--sp-7 + --sp-4` (64 px).
- Radii shrink as things nest: `--radius-lg` 14 px containers (panels, cards, stage, dialogs), `--radius-md` 8 px controls (buttons, nav items, step chips, inputs, filters), `--radius-sm` 6 px inner rows, segments, meters, icon wells, `--radius-xs` 4 px badges and chips. `--radius-pill` only where round means something: toggle tracks, dots, thin progress bars.
- Depth = opaque surface steps + a 1 px hairline + `--shadow-panel` / `--shadow-raised` (tinted with the background's hue, one light from above, a faint top inner highlight). No translucency, no `backdrop-filter`, no brand glow. Glow (`drop-shadow`) only on signals: current dots, lit LEDs, hotspots.
- Things not playable yet (coming-soon phases and topics, locked achievements) go flat: transparent fill, dashed edge.
- Background: `--bg` + 40 px graticule (matches the 1 grid unit of the circuit layout) + one faint copper "lamp" radial top left, on a fixed `body::before` (no scroll repaint). Grain: a fixed `body::after` with one 160 px SVG noise tile (`--grain`) at 5 % opacity, `pointer-events: none`, no blend mode.

| Width | Shell | Home | Lesson |

On the Build step the stage holds the wiring diagram instead (see "Build-step stage") |
| 640–1023 px | same bar | Hero stacked: copy, then bench | Mini-stage: sticky under the bar, full bleed, 45 % of the viewport (min 220 px), opaque `--bg` with a bottom rule; a 44 px bar holds the lab title and the Hide/Show toggle. Collapsed it is just that one-line bar. The step panel scrolls beneath it; `scroll-padding-top` keeps focused content clear of it |
| < 640 px | 60 px compact bar: mark only, five nav tabs as icon over an 11 px label (≥ 46 × 44 px; "Achievements" shows "Awards" but keeps its full name as `aria-label`); below 360 px icons only, every link still named | Stats as rows; day tiles one column | as tablet, plus a compact header (smaller `h1`, ring top-right, no meta line) and the step chips as one horizontally scrolling row that keeps the current chip in view; panel padding 16 px |

**Step footer:** Back (ghost) and Next (primary). On the quiz step, until the quiz is finished on this visit, Next becomes a ghost "Skip quiz", so it never competes with the quiz's own "Next question".

**Reading order:** the step panel (nav, step text, Back/Next) comes before the stage in the DOM, so screen readers and Tab reach the lesson first. Visually the stage is on the left (desktop, grid area) or pinned above the text (phones, `order: -1`).

Lessons without a lab use a single centred 760 px column. Nothing overlays lesson controls: toasts sit bottom left (phones: under the bars) for 5 s; the only modal on lesson pages is the full-screen wiring dialog, which the learner opens. Must work from 320 px with no horizontal page scroll. From 640 px, wide tables scroll inside their own focusable container, with an edge shadow while more is hidden; below 640 px each row becomes a card (first cell as its title, then "column: value" lines from `data-label`), with explicit table roles so screen readers still get a table.

## 6. Motion

- **Tokens:** `--dur-fast` (120 ms: press), `--dur-med` (220 ms: hover, colour, toggle), `--dur-slow` (420 ms: toast), `--dur-tween` (320 ms: step, route and stage changes, the chip glider), `--dur-intro` (900 ms: ring fill, home mastery bars). Easing is `--ease-out`, plus `--ease-spring` (slight overshoot) for things that travel: the chip glider and the home day ticks. All durations are 0 under reduced motion.
- **Performance rule:** animate only `transform` and `opacity` (the ring's `stroke-dashoffset` and the glider's `width` are the one-shot exceptions), and never under a `backdrop-filter`: a blurred panel over moving content is re-blurred every frame. So home panels have no backdrop blur, and each hero wave is its own `<svg>` (twice the hero's width) slid by a CSS transform on the element, a compositor-only move (moving paths inside one SVG repainted it every frame and dropped the idle home page to 30 fps). `will-change` only on the two wave layers. Measured (headless Chromium, production build): 2 s idle on home, a step change, and the stage switch run at 120/120 frames at ≤ 17 ms, at 1280 and 375 px.
- **Step change** (inside a lesson, `LessonPage`): the card leaves (`step-out`: fade, 8 px up, 40 % of `--dur-tween`), then the route changes and the new card enters (`step-in`: fade, 8 px from below, `--dur-tween`). The page does not jump. The new heading takes focus without scrolling, and only if it is out of view (above, or under the bar or mini-stage per `scroll-padding-top`) does the page glide to it (instant under reduced motion). The current-step chip (`.steps-glider`, an `aria-hidden` list item) glides from chip to chip (`transform` + `width`, `--ease-spring`). It is placed without motion on first layout and when chips reflow.
- **Page change** (home ↔ lesson ↔ achievements ↔ settings, and between lessons): a view-transition fade-through (`document.startViewTransition`, `src/app/transitions.ts`): old page out in 40 % of `--dur-tween`, new page in over the rest. The top bar is its own group (`data-nav="page"` on `<html>` while it runs), so only the current nav item crossfades. Browsers without view transitions just switch. Step changes deliberately avoid view transitions, because the snapshot costs a dropped frame.
- **Stage switch** (Wiring / Simulator): both views share one grid cell and crossfade (`opacity`, `--dur-tween`); the hidden one is `aria-hidden` + `inert` at once and `visibility: hidden` once faded. The lab stays mounted.
- **Home intro** (first load, once per browser session via `sessionStorage` `ohmlab.intro`): phase cards rise in 70 ms apart, their day ticks pop up left to right (10 ms apart), day tiles follow (45 ms apart) and their mastery bars grow from 0 (`scaleX`, `--dur-intro`). Mastery rings everywhere draw from 0 to their value on mount (`--dur-intro`).
- Micro-interactions: button press dip, tile lift on hover, toast rise (desktop) or drop (phones), meter fills.
- Ambient loops (hero waves, live dot, bench current dots) are slow and low-contrast. Current dots move at a speed proportional to the solved current, never decoratively.
- **Reduced motion:** tokens drop durations to 0, and `index.css` stops every animation (1 iteration, 0.01 ms, no delay). No view transition starts, the step exit is skipped (the route changes at once), and the home intro is not applied. Current flow also obeys the in-app "Animate current flow" setting. Nothing conveys information through motion alone; values are always printed.

## 7. Accessibility rules

- Contrast: text tokens pass WCAG AA on `--surface-1`; `--text-faint` only at ≥ 12 px for non-essential text. Never put text on a signal colour except `--brand-ink` on brand.
- Focus: every control shows a 2 px `--brand-strong` outline; custom controls drawn with box-shadow use `--glow-brand` (a 2 px gap, then a 2 px copper ring). Visually hidden inputs move the ring to their visible chip/track. Programmatic focus targets (`main`, step `h2`) don't show a ring.
- Route changes scroll to top and move focus to the step heading (`[data-autofocus]`) or `main`. Skip link "Skip to content" is the first tab stop.
- Hit targets ≥ 44 × 44 px for every button, link tile, step chip, radio chip and toast close.
- Names: follow the accessible-name contract in `docs/AGENT_TASKS.md`. Step buttons are named by the step title; their state (Done / Seen / Not seen yet) is the accessible description. Day links are "Day N: Title" with the mastery state as description. Icons are `aria-hidden`.
- Live regions: toasts (`aria-live="polite"`) and the hero bench verdict are `role="status"`. The predict reveal is not a live region; it receives focus instead.

|---|---|---|
